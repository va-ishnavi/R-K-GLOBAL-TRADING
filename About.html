WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Export Partner

RK GLOBAL TRADING is a Gujarat-based export company committed to supplying premium-quality agricultural products worldwide. We specialize in exporting Fresh Onion, Red Chilli, Pomegranate, Coconut, Cumin Seeds, Peanuts and Sesame Seeds while maintaining the highest quality standards.
